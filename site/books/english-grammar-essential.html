Unit 1: Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

We use the Present Simple for things that are true in general, or things that happen repeatedly (habits, routines, scientific facts). Examples: 'The sun rises in the east.' 'I study English every single day.'

We use the Present Continuous (am/is/are + verb-ing) for things that are happening right now at the moment of speaking, or around the present time. Examples: 'Please be quiet, I am taking an English exam right now.' 'She is living with her sister this month.'

Remember state verbs: Verbs of thinking, feeling, and senses (like, love, want, know, understand, believe, remember) are normally NOT used in continuous tenses. We say: 'I understand the lesson' (NOT: 'I am understanding').

Key Vocabulary in this Chapter

  • Routine [/ruːˈtiːn/] - A regular way of doing things (Az: Rejim, vərdiş)
  • Continuous [/kənˈtɪn.ju.əs/] - Without interruption or happening now (Az: Davam edən)
  • Stative Verb [/ˈsteɪ.tɪv vɜːb/] - A verb that describes a state rather than an action (Az: Vəziyyət bildirən fel)

Unit 2: Past Simple vs. Present Perfect

The Past Simple tells us about an action that happened at a specific, finished time in the past. It uses time words like: yesterday, in 2020, two years ago, last week, when I was a child. Example: 'I visited London in 2023.'

The Present Perfect (have/has + past participle) connects the past with the present! We use it when the time is not finished (today, this week), or when the exact time does not matter, or for life experience. Example: 'I have visited London three times in my life.'

Notice the difference: 'Did you see John this morning?' (It is now afternoon; the morning is over). 'Have you seen John this morning?' (It is still morning now).

Key Vocabulary in this Chapter

  • Specific [/spəˈsɪf.ɪk/] - Clearly defined or identified (Az: Dəqiq, xüsusi)
  • Participle [/ˈpɑː.tɪ.sɪ.pəl/] - The third form of a verb (e.g., gone, eaten) (Az: Feli sifət / 3-cü forma)
  • Experience [/ɪkˈspɪə.ri.əns/] - Knowledge or skill gained over time (Az: Təcrübə)
