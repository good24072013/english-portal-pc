Module 1: Skimming, Scanning & True/False/Not Given

Academic reading passages are over 900 words long, and you have only 20 minutes per passage. You must NOT read word-for-word. Instead, use Skimming to capture the overall gist in two minutes, and Scanning to locate specific keywords and dates.

The most common mistake in IELTS Reading is confusing 'FALSE' with 'NOT GIVEN'. Remember: 'FALSE' means the text directly contradicts the statement. 'NOT GIVEN' means the text does not contain enough information to prove or disprove the statement.

Look out for qualifying words such as always, never, sometimes, frequently, usually, possible, and probable. These small words completely transform the truth value of a claim.

Key Vocabulary in this Chapter

  • Skimming [/ˈskɪm.ɪŋ/] - Reading quickly to get the main idea (Az: Sürətlə gözdən keçirmə)
  • Contradict [/ˌkɒn.trəˈdɪkt/] - To assert the opposite of a statement (Az: Zidd olmaq, təkzib etmək)
  • Qualifying [/ˈkwɒl.ɪ.faɪ.ɪŋ/] - Limiting or moderating the meaning of another word (Az: Şərtləndirən, dəqiqləşdirən)
