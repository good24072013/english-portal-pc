Unit 1: Socializing & Introducing Yourself

A: 'Hi there! Is anyone sitting here?' B: 'No, please go ahead and take a seat. I am Sarah, by the way.' A: 'Pleasure to meet you, Sarah! I am David. Are you here for the English speaking workshop?' B: 'Yes, exactly! I am hoping to brush up on my conversational skills before starting my new job next month.'

Useful phrases for small talk: 'Do you mind if I join you?' • 'What brings you here today?' • 'How is your week going so far?' • 'I couldn't help but notice your accent—where are you from?'

Key Vocabulary in this Chapter

  • Brush up on [/brʌʃ ʌp ɒn/] - To refresh or improve knowledge of something (Az: Bilikləri təzələmək)
  • Conversational [/ˌkɒn.vəˈseɪ.ʃən.əl/] - Relating to casual talking and speech (Az: Danışıq aid olan)
  • Small talk [/smɔːl tɔːk/] - Polite, informal conversation about light topics (Az: Qeyri-rəsmi söhbət)
